What this is

A screening tool that compares the National Weather Service forecast with the personal minimums you choose. It is not an official weather briefing and doesn't replace the pilot in command's responsibility to obtain all available information before flight (14 CFR 91.103).

No warranty

Forecasts are often wrong, and the airplane numbers are typical values, not your airplane's handbook. The service is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. You decide whether and when to fly.

What we keep

Your email, home airport, minimums and airplane. We use your email only to send sign-in links and the emails you ask for. We don't sell it. Turn off the Thursday email in Settings or with the link in any email.
